Water Hammer CFD Study

A fluid-structure interaction study of pressure waves in pipes, built to capture the Joukowsky pressure rise during sudden valve closure.

What I built

  • A 2D axisymmetric monolithic FSI solver in DOLFINx using Nitsche interface coupling, k-ε turbulence closure, and a barotropic equation of state.
  • A benchmark of Crank-Nicolson versus BDF2 time integration across 9 pipe material and working fluid combinations.
  • Validation of simulated peak pressures against analytical Joukowsky theory.

Outcome

Co-authored a technical report on FSI discretization limits, scheme-dependent amplitude behavior, and compliant-pipe breakdown regimes for steel, copper, and HDPE pipes.
