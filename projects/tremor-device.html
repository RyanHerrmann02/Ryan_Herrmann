Wrist Tremor Damping Device

Biomedical senior design project: a wearable device to reduce tremor for people with essential tremor or Parkinson's disease.

What I built

  • A wrist-worn spring and air damper mechanism targeting the 5–7 Hz pathological tremor range.
  • A 3D printed enclosure, refined through 6 design revisions to tune damping performance.

Outcome

Co-authored a 24-page technical report covering design requirements, iterative development, and final prototype evaluation.
