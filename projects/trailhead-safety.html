Trailhead Safety Indicator

A low-power embedded system where the main engineering challenge was power architecture: staying asleep for weeks, then waking reliably.

What I built

  • A passive PMOS power latch using an asymmetric RC network to solve Arduino cold-start power sequencing, verified in SPICE.
  • A discrete two-stage PNP/NPN level shifter interfacing a 5 V RTC alarm signal with a 7.4 V PMOS gate while keeping the power rails isolated.

Outcome

25 days of standby battery life against a 20-day specification, with standby current cut to about 5 mA by fully powering down all non-essential components during sleep.
